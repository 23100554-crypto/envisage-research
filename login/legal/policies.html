ENVISAGE LEGAL & POLICIES

Clear expectations,
better bookings.

Effective 28 August 2026. This concise prototype policy must be reviewed by a qualified Philippine legal professional before public launch.

Platform scopeEnvisage helps clients and freelancers discover, communicate, and record booking details. It does not process payments, provide legal advice, guarantee services, or decide disputes.

TERMS OF USE

Use Envisage responsibly.

Marketplace role

Envisage is a connection and record-keeping platform. Freelancers independently provide services; clients independently choose whom to book. The parties—not Envisage—are responsible for their agreement and service performance.

Accounts and content

Users must provide accurate profile, portfolio, availability, booking, and contact information. Users may only upload content they own or have permission to use.

Booking records

The intake form, accepted booking details, and per-booking chat form the platform record. Users should review them carefully before accepting or confirming any arrangement.

Conduct and limits

Do not use Envisage for fraud, harassment, unlawful activity, false payment claims, or misuse of another person’s information. Envisage may restrict accounts that violate these rules.

PRIVACY POLICY

Only the information needed for a booking.

What we collect

We collect account and profile details, portfolios, location/service area, availability, booking details, messages, payment-status records, and QR-code images when provided by users.

Why we use it

Information is used to operate accounts, match users, manage bookings, display agreed details to the booking parties, maintain records, secure the platform, and respond to support requests.

Who can see it

Public profile and portfolio details are visible to clients. Booking, chat, agreement, payment-status, and QR-code information are visible only to the relevant client, freelancer, and authorized administrators when needed.

Storage and rights

Keep data only as long as needed for the booking, records, security, and legal obligations. Users may request access, correction, or deletion where applicable through the platform support contact.

EXTERNAL PAYMENT POLICY

Payments stay between the booking parties.

External methods only

Envisage does not collect, hold, transfer, or refund money. Freelancers may share their bank-transfer or e-wallet QR code only within an accepted booking.

Booking deposit

If both parties agree, the booking agreement may state a 50% deposit due upon acceptance and a remaining balance due on the event date. Amounts, deadlines, and cancellation terms must be shown before acceptance.

Cash balance

For an event-day cash balance, the client may mark “cash paid” and the freelancer must confirm “cash received.” Until confirmation, the payment record remains awaiting confirmation.

No guarantee or decision

Users are responsible for verifying payment details and keeping receipts. Envisage does not guarantee payment, issue refunds, act as escrow, or make legal findings in disputes.

SUPPORT & LIMITATION

Keep your records.

Envisage currently does not provide formal in-platform dispute resolution. Users should preserve their booking record and communicate directly. For legal, payment, or consumer concerns, seek appropriate independent advice or external government channels.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Good to know before you begin.

Can clients browse creatives for free?

Yes. Clients can explore local profiles, services, rates, portfolios, and availability before sending an inquiry.

Is an inquiry already a confirmed booking?

No. An inquiry opens a private conversation. A booking is requested only after both sides have discussed the details.

How do freelancers avoid double-booking?

Freelancers manage their availability and can block dates for work arranged outside Envisage.

Does Envisage process payments?

No. Payment arrangements are handled directly by the client and freelancer and recorded in the booking discussion.

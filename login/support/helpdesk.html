WHAT HAPPENS NEXT

  1. Case receivedYour report is recorded with the booking reference.
  2. Admin reviewAn administrator checks the booking intake, chat, agreement, and payment status.
  3. ClarificationThe admin may ask the client and freelancer for additional information.
  4. Outcome recordedEnvisage records any platform-rule action, such as a warning or account restriction.

Important: Envisage does not decide legal claims, order refunds, or process payments. Preserve your records for direct or external resolution.
